# Required scientific source review

The validator checks execution and specified algebra. The reviewer must examine what was actually derived. A valid graph, tool log, seeded response or match to the old engine is not by itself proof of these physical statements.

1. Identify each real sector's amplitude/operator insertion and its complete cut sum. Check transverse-spin projectors, dimensional continuation and numerator terms that multiply poles. Show Ward/gauge checks, and distinguish shared source assumptions from independent integration.
2. Derive the positive-energy delta-to-cut replacement with its sign and normalization. Identify physical cuts, measurement deltas, theta boundaries, endpoint support and i0 prescriptions separately. Establish whether each IBP relation remains valid with the measurement/regulator and whether boundary terms were included.
3. Trace actual rational numerator maps into the configured Kira target integrals, actual rules into Euler representations, and actual SubTropica results into the coefficients. Check the domain and continuation of each master, scale factors, Jacobians and expansion depth. Confirm the mutation tests act at these real dependencies.
4. Review the gauge-completed HF operator, support/EOM argument and endpoint test-function class. Do not promote vanishing contacts in the accepted physical class to a general distribution identity. Preserve the literal Yuan-Zhou/Kang CA discrepancy.
5. Review real/virtual/UV/collinear/rapidity subtraction allocation, delta and plus terms, and the finite matching scheme. Confirm the UU/UT relative Collins sign, masses, z powers and Fourier measures. SIDIS conventions are the baseline where applicable; polarized extensions need explicit definitions.
6. Review the jet measurement, overlap and narrow-cone approximation. A zero extra finite jet remainder must follow from the calculated terms. Neither that zero nor a new integration route establishes validity at R=1 or the omitted nonsingular recoil terms.
7. Check formal UU/UT assembly at strict first order and the operator/convolution measures. Fitted functions stay symbolic. The individual coefficient comparisons and final assembly must share the same native inputs.
8. Retained MadGraph results validate the preserved amplitude implementation. Bind any new/modified amplitude or spin adapter to an appropriate direct check, including helicity interference for transverse spin. Until done, record that qualification rather than quoting old check counts as new coverage.
9. Preserve all historical analytical comparison groups, including mismatches and unverified rows. Distinguish derived/imported/model inputs, algebraic equality, explicit conversion, numerical checks and scientific scope. Keep the analytical and supporting numerical PDFs separate.

The method references are the pinned SIDIS workflow and its notes, with reverse unitarity discussed in https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0207004. The observable and perturbative assumptions remain those of https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.07281v1, read together with https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.00913 and the component/operator references preserved in the retained package. These references are comparisons and method guidance, not substitutes for freshly evaluating the new route's integrals.
