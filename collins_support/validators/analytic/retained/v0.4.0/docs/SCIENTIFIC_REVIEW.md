# Required scientific source review

Keep this review separate from automatic row counts. For each new obligation, record DERIVED / EXTERNAL / MODEL origin, the actual producing code and intermediates, independent evidence, mathematical agreement and physical scope. Do not call a comparison to an imported formula a derivation.

1. Trace the independent HF operator, its imaginary/chiral-odd projection, Wilson boundary, EOM and normalization into the real diagrams. Check the D-dimensional projection and regulator order, not just its four-dimensional limit. Verify which UV and IR poles are separated before matching.
2. Trace the bulk mixing term and both endpoint/contact components into Hhat evolution. Compare both literal primary sources after the stated conversions. Explain the CA-numerator discrepancy recorded in `references.json`; do not silently change either quote.
3. Inspect the finite HF calculation and its independent route. In particular, identify the O(eps) numerator information multiplying a transverse pole. Demonstrate that both routes do not simply reuse the same final finite export. The frozen bulk kernel and RG derivative do not fix all finite constants or endpoint contacts.
4. Verify the full regulated distribution action, including the change of inverse-fraction variables and PV/endpoint extension. HF(z,z)=0 does not by itself permit dropping an arbitrary derivative contact. Check actual support, crossed pieces when required, and boundary terms from integration by parts.
5. Trace the finite fragmenting-jet coefficient through the actual ep measurement and overlap subtraction. Prove the scalar quark relation and its spin independence in the specified organization. Explain why the inclusive pp out-of-jet hard coefficient is or is not relevant; do not import it because its name is similar.
6. State precisely where the narrow-cone expansion enters. Distinguish exact agreement with printed A1–A3 from a validity argument at R=1. Do not label a single geometric correction as a complete renormalized finite-R correction.
7. Trace the d17/d18 coefficients into d19's actual native observable. Check the full all-b operator expression separately from the local small-b OPE, the independent Fourier variables, charge/flavor sum, Collins sign and mass/z factors, and the ratio after the same integration functional.
8. Inspect both upstream probes in source and regenerated intermediates. They must perturb the regulated numerator and propagate through native calculations. Editing a final coefficient directly would invalidate their derivation-provenance interpretation even if the software comparisons pass.
9. Keep the paper approximation distinguishable from the additional HF/finite-jet terms. No fitted matrix element is needed to derive their coefficients. State the absence of a closed full twist-three evolution system, unrestricted nonsingular recoil, a finite-R certificate, fitting and Figure 6 validation.

**Known testing limit:** a common wrong finite constant inserted into both purportedly independent routes can satisfy every pole/RG identity and both routes' algebra. The software self-test demonstrates this rather than disguising it. Native trace review and a genuinely independent calculation are indispensable. Similarly, a matching bulk kernel does not by itself settle the endpoint extension or reference-convention discrepancy.

The implemented two-fraction/jet transport is specific to the chosen leading-power quark factorization. If the derivation establishes a required structure that does not fit it, save the native result and a concrete proposed contract/oracle extension for independent review. Do not discard the structure or relabel it as a model input to pass.
