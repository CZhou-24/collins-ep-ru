Coverage labels: **whole** means the entire declared group comparison expression (with its stripped factors); **part** means a checked component or bound input to a review operation; **definition** means an operator, tree or formal-algebra identity; **conditional zero** means a representative on the recorded domain; **unmapped** means no established group relation. A group link lists its paper references below; it does not assert literature agreement.
