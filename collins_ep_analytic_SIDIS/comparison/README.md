# Output and equation comparison

[OUTPUT_TO_EQUATION_INDEX.md](OUTPUT_TO_EQUATION_INDEX.md) and its [JSON counterpart](OUTPUT_TO_EQUATION_INDEX.json) map the 167 scalar reference outputs onto the broader 85 equation groups. The unchanged [comparison PDFs and qualifications](../reports/README.md) distinguish exact comparisons, explicit conversions and unresolved groups.

Historical absolute paths in the index identify original evidence; they are provenance, not current filesystem requirements. The portable comparison targets and their recorded origins are in [reference_values.json](../../collins_support/validators/analytic/reference_values.json). Reference equations and source details are in [references.json](../../collins_support/validators/analytic/references.json).

Current r00–r07 runs derive new native exports and compare them against those targets. See [execution commands](../../collins_support/README.md). Matching the previous engine does not independently verify every equation or physical assumption.
