## Why the counts differ

All 167 accepted scalar comparisons pass, while 35 whole groups remain UNVERIFIED against the old engine. A scalar may check only a subexpression, normalization definition or physical-class representative within a broader equation. Some groups require operator assumptions, unresolved contacts or unexported ingredients. Several exports alias one native coefficient; the zero jet labels follow one cancellation. Neither partial coverage nor repeated scalar aliases upgrades a whole equation to EXACT.

| Whole-group comparison, 85 groups each | EXACT | EXACT AFTER EXPLICIT CONVERSION | MISMATCH | UNVERIFIED |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Historical statuses, preserved | 14 | 35 | 4 | 32 |
| Accepted RU versus old engine | 42 | 8 | 0 | 35 |
| Accepted RU versus published converted target | 0 | 23 | 0 | 62 |

The historical electromagnetic-prefactor, printed Sudakov and literal CA-kernel discrepancies remain visible. A separately checked C16 CA-kernel subexpression is MISMATCH even though the broader C16 group remains qualified; the index does not turn that into a whole-group PASS. Printed equations and proposed corrections/conversions remain separate.

The numerical report distinguishes unchanged regenerated amplitudes bound to retained MadGraph/complex-helicity evidence from new direct spacelike/TMD/HF algebra, Clifford and regulated complex-current checks. It does not describe those new HF checks as a newly generated MadGraph process. Native master/reduction probes and retained baseline replay are separately identified.
